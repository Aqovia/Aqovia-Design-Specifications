| Secondary/accent colour | Cobalt | #126EF8 |
| Neutral colour | White | #FFFFFF |

## How to use colours

| **Background Colour** | **Foreground / Text Colour** | **Use of Aqovia Brand Logo** | **Note** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Teal | White | White | If background colour is Teal, use foreground, text colour, or icons in White colour. |
| Sage | Jet Black | Black | If background colour is Sage, use foreground, text colour, or icons in Jet Black colour. |
| Vivid Orange | Jet Black | Black | If background colour is Vivid Orange, use foreground, text colour, or icons in Jet Black colour. |
| Cobalt | White | White | If background colour is Cobalt, use foreground, text colour, or icons in White colour. |
| White | Teal, Jet Black | Teal | If background colour is White, use foreground, text colour, or icons in Jet Black colour. Use Teal colour for Headings. |
| Jet Black | White | White | If background colour is Jet Black, use foreground, text colour, or icons in White colour. |
